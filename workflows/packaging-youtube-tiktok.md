# Packaging: titles, descriptions, tags (template)

Fill in the `<...>` parts once, then reuse. This is the instruction set the creator would otherwise paste into a chat AI for each long-form video. Use it for the same job here, and for shorts whenever asked ("short description" = the small version at the end).

## Principles
- Accurate to the supplied title, thumbnail and facts from the video. Natural, concise, creator voice. No exaggeration, no invented events, no unsupported claims (records, rankings, stats) unless the creator or the video itself states them. No emojis, filler or generic AI wording. Do not ask follow-up questions; decide from context.
- The working title is a starting point; keep distinctive wording or humour. No platform named = YouTube only. Only generate the platforms and sections asked for. Revisions change only the part asked.
- Research (patch notes, community terms) only when it clearly improves the specific video; leave it out of the output unless it helps the title or description.

## YouTube (long form), sections in this order
1. **Recommended title** (searchable, subject name when useful, real hook, natural, keeps the creator's humour).
2. **3 to 5 alternative titles** (different approaches: search-focused, content-focused, humorous, curiosity without misleading).
3. **Description**: 1 to 3 short sentences summary; a relevant verified context line only if useful; 3 to 5 hashtags at the end of the main text; social links; gear list with referral links (if the creator has one); affiliate disclosure. Short lines, separated sections, ready to paste; no alternative titles or tags inside it.
4. **SEO tags**: separate comma-separated list (topic, names, format, title variations); no hashtags, no unrelated names, no repetition.

## TikTok / Instagram / Twitch (only when asked)
- TikTok: short direct caption, small set of hashtags, social links.
- Instagram: short Reel caption, at most 5 hashtags, no links unless asked.
- Twitch: only the requested piece (stream title, stream description, channel description); do not invent a schedule.

## Social links (always exactly as written)
```
YouTube: <your channel URL>
TikTok: <your profile URL>
Twitch: <your channel URL>
Discord: <your invite URL>
```

## Gear list block (optional; copy exactly)
```
My Setup:

<Part type>: <Product name>
<referral or product link>

<Part type>: <Product name>
<referral or product link>

Some links may be affiliate links, which means I may earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you.
```
Do not claim every linked product is an exact match for the owned hardware if the links are referral or close matches. No extra explanation about the links.

## Shorts version ("small")
Per short: one title, a 1 to 2 line description from facts in the video, 3 to 5 hashtags, the social links. No gear list or affiliate block unless asked. Facts only from what the creator said or what is on screen.
